Proposal

Give support the European morning

One hire in Lisbon covers the six hours that currently have nobody in them. It costs us the help-centre rewrite, and I think that is the right trade.

01The problem

Nobody answers Europe before 13:00 CET. Just under a third of our tickets arrive in that window and wait a median of six hours and ten minutes for a first reply — not a resolution, a reply.

We cannot rota our way out of it — the US team already starts early and the median has not moved in three quarters. Two of the five enterprise accounts we lost this year named response time in their exit call. Both were European.

02What I propose

One support engineer in Lisbon, starting January, covering 08:00–17:00 CET. On our rotation, in our tooling, in the same queue — not an outsourced tier one and not a follow-the-sun handover. A named person who is awake when the tickets arrive. Lisbon because we already have an entity there, so hiring runs about ten weeks instead of the four months an entity setup would add.

03What it costs

  • Salary and employer costs, band S3$88,000
  • Tooling seats and equipment$3,400
  • Recruiting fee, internal$2,000
  • First-year total$93,400

No new vendor, no new process, no change to the on-call contract. Finance has reviewed the band and confirmed it sits inside the FY27 envelope.

04What it buys

Modelled on the last eight months of volume, first response in the window falls from six hours ten to under forty-five minutes — 31% of volume moves from our worst response band to our best.

It also gives the four European accounts renewing in Q2 — $340k of ARR — a named contact in their own working day. Three of the four have asked for one.

05What we give up

The help-centre rewrite, the other claim on this budget. It is worth doing; I am arguing about order, not merit. The rewrite is modelled to cut ticket volume by about 8%. The hire changes the experience of 31%, and it changes it for the accounts that are actually leaving.

And if the hire lands, the rewrite becomes a stronger Q3 proposal: we will have someone in the timezone who knows which articles are missing.

06The ask

Approve the requisition, at band S3, against the Q1 contractor budget.

Ten weeks of hiring makes 30 September the last date that still produces a January start. If the answer is no, I would rather have it by the 30th than have it open — I will put the money into the rewrite and stop making the case.
