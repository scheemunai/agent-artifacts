Why not the others

  • A · Hard cap with an upgrade prompt

    Cheapest, fastest, and the only option guaranteeing no unbilled usage — a real argument. It is also the only one that stops a customer mid-export. Two of our four largest accounts would have hit the cap in July, and neither can close procurement in a day.

  • C · Soft cap with a seven-day grace

    The compromise, and it collects most of B's revenue. But a grace period still ends, and it ends on a Tuesday for somebody. It also needs its own notifications — so it costs nearly what B costs and keeps A's problem.

What we are asking for

  1. Approve Option B and a five-week build starting 22 September.
  2. Accept that the first invoice carrying overage lands on 3 February, not in January.
  3. Name a rollback owner who is not the person building it — the revert is two weeks and needs someone free to call it.
